<objective>
Stop the lookup callables (`getReservationForItem`, `hydrateActiveReservation`) from silently returning `{active: null}` when a reservation has an empty `affiliateUrl`. This currently makes any URL-imported, non-EMAG item (e.g. IKEA) unreachable on the dedicated per-item reserve page — the page falls through to the "This isn't your reservation" branch even though the reservation is real and the caller IS the giver.

Purpose: Restore reach-ability of non-EMAG reservations across page refresh, new tab, and the per-item URL pattern. Keeps server as the single source of truth for `affiliateUrl` and avoids client-side fan-out logic.

Output:
- Both callables read the item doc (they already do for `itemName` + `merchantDomain`), pick up `originalUrl`, and return `effectiveAffiliateUrl = reservation.affiliateUrl || item.originalUrl || ""` in the response.
- The two web surfaces that render the "Continue to retailer" anchor hide it when `active.affiliateUrl === ''`. Release + countdown + confirm-purchase flows are untouched.
- Tests inverted and extended to lock in the new behavior on both ends.
</objective>
