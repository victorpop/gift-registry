## Decisions Made

- Reused `active.reservationId` + `active.itemId` at the navigate call site rather than plumbing the values through a ref. `active` is already in scope at the effect, and the guard `if (!active) return` immediately above the navigate guarantees non-null at the call site. This matches the j5j payload-shape exactly (k-21 uses `active!.reservationId` and `itemId` — here `active.itemId` is preferred because `itemId` from `useParams` is `string | undefined` whereas `active.itemId` is statically `string`).
- Kept the comment block above the effect to document WHY both racers carry the payload — without it a future maintainer might delete the "duplicate" state payload thinking the release-success effect covers it.
